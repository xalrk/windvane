# T-004: Habits with a weekly target
Depends on: T-003.
Touches: habits.py, tests/test_target.py.

## Goal
Some habits aren't daily: the gym three times a week is kept up even with rest days between. Counted as a daily
streak it looks broken every other day.

## Scope
A habit can have a target of N days a week, weeks running Monday to Sunday. `add`, `list` and `streak` learn it;
`done` and `week` treat it like any other habit, and daily habits are unchanged.

## Done when
- `add gym --per-week 3` adds the line `habit gym 3/week` and prints `added gym (3 a week)`; a `--per-week` that isn't a whole number from 1 to 7 prints `error: bad target: <value>` and exits 2. A hand-written `habit gym 3/week` line is read the same way, and one with a target outside 1 to 7 is a bad line.
- `list` prints a weekly habit as `- gym: <N> days (3 a week)`.
- `streak` counts a weekly habit in weeks: a week counts when at least N of its days are done. The current streak is the run of counted weeks that ends this week, or that ends last week when this week hasn't reached N yet; the best is the longest run ever, across year ends. It prints `gym: <N> weeks, best <M>` (`1 week` for one, `0 weeks` for none).
- tests in `tests/test_target.py` cover each point.
