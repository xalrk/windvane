# T-004: Show spoon, cup and count quantities as kitchen fractions
Depends on: T-003.
Touches: recipes.py, tests/test_fractions.py.

## Goal
Halving a recipe gives `0.33 cup` and `1.5 eggs`; nobody's measuring cup says `0.33`. Cooks read spoons, cups and
counts as fractions, and grams and millilitres as numbers.

## Scope
How `show`, `scale` and `shop` print a quantity with no unit, or in `tsp`, `tbsp` or `cup`. Quantities in `g`, `kg`,
`ml` and `l`, and the serves line, keep T-001's form.

## Done when
- a quantity with no unit or in `tsp`, `tbsp` or `cup` that is within 0.01 of a whole number plus halves, thirds, quarters or eighths prints as that fraction in lowest terms: `1/2 tsp`, `1 1/2 cup`, `2/3 cup`, `3/8 tsp`, `1 1/2 eggs`; a whole number still prints as `2`.
- one that isn't near such a fraction keeps T-001's form (`0.2 tsp` shows as `0.2 tsp`).
- quantities in `g`, `kg`, `ml` and `l` keep T-001's form (`0.5 kg` stays `0.5 kg`), and so does the serves line (`serves 1.5`).
- this holds in `show`, `scale` and `shop` alike: a recipe with `1 cup milk` scaled `--by 1/3` prints `- 1/3 cup milk`, and two recipes with `1/4 tsp salt` each make `- 1/2 tsp salt` in `shop`.
- tests in `tests/test_fractions.py` cover each point.
