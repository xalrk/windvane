# T-002: Keys and locked doors
Depends on: T-001.
Touches: game.py, tests/test_keys.py.

## Goal
A maze with nothing in it is a walk. Keys and the doors they open are the puzzle.

## Scope
A key is picked up by stepping on it; each key opens one door.

## Done when
- stepping on a key `k` prints `You pick up a key.` after the position line, and the square becomes floor.
- moving into a door `D` with no key prints `The door is locked.` and the player stays where they were.
- moving into a door with a key prints `You unlock the door.` and then the position line: the key is used up and the door's square becomes floor.
- `inventory` prints `Keys: <n>`.
- tests in `tests/test_keys.py` cover each point.
