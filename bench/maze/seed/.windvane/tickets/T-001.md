# T-001: Move around the maze
Depends on: nothing.
Touches: game.py, tests/test_move.py.

## Goal
The game starts and quits, but the player can't move. Moving and looking around is the whole game's base.

## Scope
Four directions and `look`. Keys, doors and the exit come in later tickets: until then `k`, `D` and `E` are floor.

## Done when
- `n`, `s`, `e` and `w` (or `north`, `south`, `east`, `west`) move one square and print `You are at (x, y).`
- a move into a wall `#` or off the map prints `You can't go that way.` and the player stays where they were.
- `look` prints the map's rows as loaded, with `@` on the player's square and the start square shown as `.`
- tests in `tests/test_move.py` cover each point.
